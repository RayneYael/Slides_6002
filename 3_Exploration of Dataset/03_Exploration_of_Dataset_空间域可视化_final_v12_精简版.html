Speaker notes

The treemap is a hierarchical part-to-whole comparison. Area represents each country’s share of 2021 global CO₂, while colour groups countries by region. Asia contributes 59.8% of global emissions, China alone contributes 31.4%, and the Top 20 countries account for 81.2%. The smallest cells are intentionally left unlabelled. The statistics were checked against the final 2021 panel.

Speaker notes

Land fill represents total CO₂ on a log₁₀ megatonne scale, while 3-D bar height and colour represent CO₂ per person. China leads total emissions, whereas Qatar has the highest per-capita value. Natural Earth 110m polygons match 169 of 213 panel entities but still cover approximately 99.6% of global emissions. Some small islands and territories may therefore be absent from land fill.

Speaker notes

The categorical choropleth shows each country’s dominant fossil-fuel source in 2021: coal, oil or gas. Pie charts provide the actual three-fuel shares for selected major emitters, preventing the dominant-category map from hiding mixed national profiles. White areas indicate missing or unreliable fuel profiles. The figure compares spatial regimes and fuel structure, not the magnitude of total emissions.
