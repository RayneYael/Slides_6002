Read the pipeline from left to right: country rows are filtered to 1992–2021, joined by ISO-3 and year, completed under an OWID-first rule, then used to derive analytical fields. Each transformation depends on the previous one, while the central provenance bar shows why the supplemental dataset materially improves fuel coverage. The resulting panel contains 6,378 country-year rows, 213 ISO-coded entities and 30 years. Core measures are complete, but some small territories and unreliable fuel profiles require cautious interpretation.
