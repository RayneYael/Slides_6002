Feature engineering and modelling pipeline

11 ENGINEERED FEATURES
01

Level

  • Recent log per-capita CO₂

Captures current intensity without allowing extreme values to dominate distance.

02

Direction

  • Long-term trend
  • Recent trend
  • Acceleration

Separates long-run direction from recent slowdown, reversal or renewed growth.

03

Timing

  • Growth volatility
  • Peak timing
  • Post-peak change

Describes when change occurs and whether the trajectory is smooth or unstable.

04

Fuel shift

  • Recent coal / gas shares
  • Coal / gas share change

Oil is retained for interpretation but omitted from the model to avoid perfect dependence.

No supervised accuracy

Accuracy, Precision, Recall and F1 require ground-truth labels; they are not applicable here.

Silhouette

Rewards compact clusters that remain separated from neighbouring clusters. Higher is better.

DB + CH

Davies–Bouldin penalises overlap; Calinski–Harabasz rewards between-cluster separation.

Inertia caveat

Inertia falls as K increases, so it cannot select K on its own.
